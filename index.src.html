Who we are

A real home, run on kindness.

The Ready To Serve Foundation runs a free old age home in Vanasthalipuram, Hyderabad for elders who have been left with no family, no income and nowhere to go.

Every rupee you give buys real things our residents need today — hot meals, medicines, clothes, a clean bed, and the joy of being remembered on their birthday.

  • Three nutritious meals, every single day
  • Medicines & regular health check-ups
  • Birthday celebrations so no elder feels forgotten
  • Run by volunteers — donations reach the home, not overheads
